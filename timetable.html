Important Information

Booking

All classes can be booked through your Member Dashboard or by contacting us directly.

What to Bring

Please bring your own water bottle and towel. Gi (uniform) is required for Jiu-jitsu, Karate and Judo classes. Muay Thai requires gloves and shin guards (available to hire).

Private Tuition

Private tuition sessions are available throughout the week at £15 per hour. Contact us to book a one-on-one session with any of our instructors.
