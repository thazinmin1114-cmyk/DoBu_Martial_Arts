About DoBu Martial Arts

DoBu Martial Arts is a premier martial arts gym dedicated to helping students of all ages and skill levels achieve their fitness and self-defence goals. With experienced instructors, world-class facilities, and a supportive community, we provide a training environment where everyone can thrive.

Our Story

History of DoBu Martial Arts

Founded by Mauricio Gomez, a 4th Dan Blackbelt in Judo and experienced martial arts coach, DoBu Martial Arts was established with a simple mission: to provide high-quality martial arts training in a welcoming and inclusive environment. What started as a small training space has grown into a fully-equipped martial arts facility offering a wide range of classes for all ages and skill levels.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower individuals through martial arts training, helping them develop physical strength, mental discipline, and self-confidence. We believe that martial arts is for everyone, regardless of age, gender, or fitness level, and we are committed to providing a safe and supportive environment where students can learn, grow, and achieve their goals.

Our Core Values

  • Respect - We show respect to our instructors, fellow students, and the martial arts traditions we practice.
  • Discipline - We believe in the importance of consistent training, hard work, and dedication to achieving our goals.
  • Excellence - We strive for excellence in everything we do, from technique and fitness to customer service and facility maintenance.
  • Community - We foster a strong sense of community, where students support and encourage each other on their martial arts journey.

Our Facilities

Our fully-equipped facility provides everything you need for effective martial arts training and fitness.

Training Mat Area

A large, matted martial arts area providing a safe and spacious environment for all martial arts training, including Jiu-jitsu, Judo, Karate, and Muay Thai.

Weights & Fitness Area

A fully-equipped gym with free weights, resistance machines, cardio equipment, and functional training areas to support your fitness and conditioning goals.

Changing Rooms

Spacious and clean changing and shower facilities with secure lockers, ensuring students can freshen up before and after training sessions.

Sauna

A traditional wooden sauna to help with post-training recovery, muscle relaxation, and overall wellbeing after intense martial arts sessions.

Steam Room

A modern steam room providing therapeutic heat treatment to aid muscle recovery, improve circulation, and promote relaxation after training.
