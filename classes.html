Our Classes

We offer a wide range of martial arts and fitness classes for all skill levels. From complete beginners to experienced practitioners, there is something for everyone at DoBu Martial Arts.

Jiu-jitsu

Brazilian Jiu-jitsu is a martial art and combat sport that focuses on ground fighting and submission techniques. It teaches practitioners how to use leverage and technique to overcome larger, stronger opponents. Our Jiu-jitsu classes cover both gi and no-gi training, including takedowns, positional control, submissions, and live sparring.

Key Benefits:

  • Ground control and positional dominance
  • Submission techniques (chokes and joint locks)
  • Effective self-defence for real-world situations
  • Improved flexibility, strength, and cardiovascular fitness
  • Confidence and mental resilience

Suitable Skill Levels:

Beginner Intermediate Advanced

Karate

Traditional Shotokan Karate training builds striking power, speed, coordination, and discipline. Our classes cover fundamental techniques (kihon), forms (kata), and sparring (kumite). Karate is an excellent martial art for building character, improving focus, and developing effective self-defence skills. We offer separate classes for children and adults.

Key Benefits:

  • Powerful striking techniques (punches, kicks, blocks)
  • Improved coordination, balance, and reflexes
  • Discipline, focus, and mental strength
  • Effective self-defence skills
  • Character development and respect

Suitable Skill Levels:

Beginner Intermediate Advanced
View Timetable

Judo

Judo is a modern martial art and Olympic sport that emphasises throws, takedowns, and groundwork. Our Judo classes develop balance, coordination, physical conditioning, and confidence through dynamic training. Students learn safe falling techniques (ukemi), throwing techniques (nage-waza), and ground techniques (katame-waza) in a safe and supportive environment.

Key Benefits:

  • Dynamic throwing and takedown techniques
  • Improved balance, coordination, and physical conditioning
  • Confidence through practical self-defence
  • Safe falling skills that protect against injury
  • Competitive and recreational opportunities

Suitable Skill Levels:

Beginner Intermediate Advanced
View Timetable

Muay Thai

Muay Thai, also known as the art of eight limbs, is a striking martial art that combines punches, kicks, elbows, and knees. Our Muay Thai classes provide a full-body workout that improves cardiovascular fitness, strength, flexibility, and coordination. Students learn pad work, bag work, clinch techniques, and controlled sparring under the guidance of our experienced instructor.

Key Benefits:

  • Full-body workout using all eight limbs
  • Improved cardiovascular fitness and endurance
  • Powerful striking techniques (punches, kicks, elbows, knees)
  • Clinch work and close-range combat skills
  • Stress relief and mental toughness

Suitable Skill Levels:

Beginner Intermediate Advanced

Fitness & Self-Defence Courses

Fitness Training

Our fully equipped private fitness gym is available to all members, with dedicated personal training sessions available for those who want one-on-one guidance. The fitness gym features free weights, resistance machines, cardio equipment, and functional training areas.

Self-Defence Course

Our six-week Self-Defence course is designed to teach practical, effective self-defence techniques that can be applied in real-world situations. The course covers awareness and prevention, basic striking techniques, escapes from common holds and grabs, ground defence, and scenario-based training.
