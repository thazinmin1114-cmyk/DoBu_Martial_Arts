Welcome to DoBu Martial Arts

At DoBu Martial Arts, we believe that martial arts training is about more than just learning to fight. It is about building confidence, discipline, physical fitness and mental strength. Our experienced instructors are dedicated to helping every member reach their full potential, whether you are a complete beginner or an experienced practitioner.

With a wide range of classes throughout the week, flexible membership options and a friendly, supportive community, there has never been a better time to start your martial arts journey.

Our Classes

We offer four main martial arts disciplines, plus Self-Defence and Private Fitness training. Each class is taught by qualified and experienced instructors.

Jiu-jitsu

Brazilian Jiu-jitsu focuses on ground fighting and submission techniques. Learn leverage and control to overcome larger opponents.

Key Benefits: Ground control, submission skills, self-defence | Suitable for: All levels
Learn More about Jiu-jitsu

Karate

Traditional Karate training builds striking power, speed and discipline through kata, kumite and fundamental techniques.

Key Benefits: Striking power, discipline, coordination | Suitable for: All levels
Learn More about Karate

Judo

Judo emphasises throws, takedowns and groundwork. Develop balance, coordination and confidence through dynamic training.

Key Benefits: Throws, balance, physical conditioning | Suitable for: All levels
Learn More about Judo

Muay Thai

The art of eight limbs. Muay Thai combines punches, kicks, elbows and knees for a full-body striking workout.

Key Benefits: Full-body workout, striking skills, fitness | Suitable for: All levels
Learn More about Muay Thai
